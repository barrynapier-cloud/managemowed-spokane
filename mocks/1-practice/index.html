7 Days to Launch a Clinical Peptide Program in Your Practice.

A real playbook, not a sales asset. We've helped clinicians launch dozens of these programs. This is the path that actually works.

  1. Day 01

    Choose your initial peptide lineup.

    Six to ten peptides keyed to your demographic. Not the catalog — the focused lineup that will sell, ship, and produce results in your specific practice.

  2. Day 02

    Build your bundles.

    Weight loss · aesthetics · longevity · neurocognitive · TRT add-ons. Demographic-tuned, priced for your market, written so a patient understands the why.

  3. Day 03

    Set up the ordering rail.

    Practitioner ordering portal configured. Optional custom-branded in-clinic app for patient ordering and education. Front-desk approval flow standardized.

  4. Day 04

    Patient education and informed consent.

    Pre-crafted, compliant documentation. Compound identification, batch numbers, treatment rationale. Consent that reads like a partnership, not a liability waiver.

  5. Day 05

    The first email to your existing list.

    Sequence written. The right way to introduce peptides to patients who already trust you, without sounding like a supplement bro or a stranger.

  6. Day 06

    Pricing that competes on quality, not price.

    You will not win on price against an Amazon vial. You will win on what's in the bottle, what's behind it, and who's watching the labs. We price to that reality.

  7. Day 07

    Go live with your first five patients.

    Real protocols, real results, real cash flow. Then we iterate the lineup based on what your patients actually keep coming back for.

No drip campaign, no nurture sequence. The PDF and a one-line follow-up. That's it.
