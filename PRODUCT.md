# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Clinician owners — MDs, medical directors, and med spa owners who run a working
practice and want to launch or expand a clinical peptide program. Already
successful operators looking for the marketing, education, legal-compliance,
and clinical playbook to add a new high-margin service line that produces real
results for their patients.

## Product Purpose

BMB is a practice growth consultancy that helps clinics implement clinical
peptide programs end-to-end — legally compliant, profitable, and clinically
effective. The site's job is to convert qualified practice owners into leads,
primarily by offering a substantial downloadable guide ("7 Days to Launch a
Clinical Peptide Program in Your Practice"), with "book a call" as the
secondary CTA.

## Brand Personality

The two-pillar voice: a 22-year MD with active clinic experience, paired with a
brand/marketing/peptide-content expert. Authoritative without being sterile.
Warm without being soft. Clinical without being corporate. Three words:
**Authoritative · Alive · Grounded.**

## Anti-references

- Bro-supplement / bodybuilder aesthetic (black + neon, hyped headlines, "stack" energy)
- Wellness cliché (sage greens, soft gradients, meditation stock photography)
- Generic medical stock (stethoscope shots, clip-art doctors, white + teal default)
- MLM / get-rich-quick coaching vibes ("I made $500k, you can too")
- AI-generated slop — generic gradients, hero-metric template, identical card grids
- Anything that feels low-effort or under-considered

## Design Principles

1. **Two voices, one brand.** Clinical authority and marketing energy together
   — never picked between. The MD's experience grounds it; the coach's
   fluency makes it move. If a section feels like only one voice, it's wrong.

2. **Show the work, not the promise.** Credibility through specifics — real
   practices, real compliance frameworks, real numbers, real names. No vague
   "transform your practice" language; no unsubstantiated claims.

3. **Practitioner peers.** The reader is an MD or owner who's already
   successful. Speak as a peer who has done this in a real clinic — not a
   guru explaining basics, not a sales rep pitching features.

4. **Modern medical, alive.** The Hims / Forward / Function Health lane —
   editorial, confident, energetic. Real photography over stock. Color used
   on purpose. Never sterile, never bro, never wellness-cliché.

5. **The lead magnet earns the click.** The downloadable guide is the front
   door of the funnel. Its presentation on the site — and the asset itself —
   must feel as substantial as the brand around it.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA as baseline (sensible default for a B2B medical brand; legible
type, sufficient contrast, keyboard navigation, reduced-motion respect). No
additional accommodations specified.
